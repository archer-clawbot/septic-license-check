Licensing Data

The Septic Guide publishes aggregate counts of septic providers matched to state license lists, by state and county, without individual business records. The dataset is archived on Zenodo with a DOI (10.5281/zenodo.23550643), and the septic data page explains the 26 state license lists used. Methods are on how licenses are verified.
